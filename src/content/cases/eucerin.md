  - heading: Context
    body: >-
      Beiersdorf (Nivea's parent company) was entering a new market with Eucerin — a
      dermatology-focused brand with strong European equity but no local recognition.
      Our team was brought in as the Digital & SMM partner to build the brand's digital
      presence from the ground up.

  - heading: Challenge
    body: >-
      Eucerin sits at the intersection of beauty and medicine, a category with strict
      visual and messaging guidelines. The challenge was to adapt global brand
      communications for a local audience without losing the scientific credibility that
      defines the brand. Everything — content, visuals, tone — had to be built from scratch.
    images:
      - eucerin-challenge-1.png
      - eucerin-challenge-2.png
  - heading: Strategy
    body: >-
      I led the project as the brand lead, responsible for brand communications strategy
      and cross-functional coordination. The core strategic decision was to treat the brand
      not as a beauty label but as a science and skincare authority and build all content
      around that positioning. I developed a content policy that balanced scientific
      authority with accessible, lifestyle-driven storytelling, created a local visual
      identity — minimalist, lab-inspired — that worked within global brand guidelines
      while standing out in a cluttered beauty feed, adapted all communications and
      guidelines for the local audience including materials for dermatologists and pharmacy
      staff, and built a structured approach to community engagement to grow a loyal and
      active audience base.
    images:
      - eucerin-strategy-1.png
      - eucerin-strategy-2.png
  - heading: Execution
    body: >-
      Launched and maintained brand channels across Instagram and YouTube. Organized local
      photo productions — minimalist, product-focused shoots with scientific and laboratory
      visual elements. Produced Stories series covering product lines, ingredients, brand
      history, and skin conditions. Adapted content for multiple touchpoints: social media,
      healthcare professionals, and point of sale.
  - heading: ""
    body: >-
      Built engagement mechanics to bring new audiences into conversation with the brand.
      Worked with opinion leaders in the beauty and dermatology space. Maintained consistent
      expert Q&A formats to deepen audience trust. During COVID lockdowns, coordinated fully
      remote production — one photographer on set, remote supervision by the team — to keep
      publishing without interruption.
