  - heading: Strategy
    body: >-
      I took the brand name itself as the creative spark. "Philips" sounds like "Feel" — and that
      became the platform idea: Feel the Music. Not just hear it, feel it. This insight shaped
      everything: the visual language, the tone of voice, the content pillars, and the influencer
      approach. Instead of talking about specs, we talked about music as an experience. We
      positioned Philips Audio as a brand for people who are genuinely obsessed with sound,
      curious about its history, excited by experiments, and always chasing the next great listen.

  - heading: Execution
    body: >-
      We launched a full brand platform across social media with a visual style built around music
      culture. Content covered stories from the music world, sound experiments, unusual genres,
      artist conversations, and curated collections of festivals, concerts, films, and tracks. To
      bring the platform to life authentically, we partnered with Z-generation bloggers who
      created native content that felt organic to their audiences. Over 70 influencer placements
      ran between June 2019 and September 2020, alongside a full suite of social media promo
      materials.
